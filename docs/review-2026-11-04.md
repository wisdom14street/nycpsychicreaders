## Measurement contract

| Signal | What it establishes | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GA4 page views / sessions | Recorded website traffic when the tag runs | Unique paying customers or attendance |
| `booking_click`, `phone_click`, `email_click`, `directions_click`, `claim_click`, `shop_click` | A visitor activated a link | An appointment, call connection, submitted claim, visit or sale |
| `add_to_cart` | Quantity added to the local browser cart | Shopify payment or an order |
| `begin_checkout` | A nonempty cart was handed toward Shopify checkout | A completed or paid order |
| Shopify order with Storefront/Site/ref markers | A resulting order can be assigned to this storefront, subject to marker retention | Payment success without checking financial status, or a Square booking |
| Square confirmed appointment / payment record | Booking/payment evidence from the booking provider | Attribution to this site unless a supported source marker is retained and verified |

No `purchase` or booking-completion event is invented here. Do not mark these click events as paid-sale conversions. Historical `generate_lead` values from the old implementation are click counts and must be labeled accordingly. Use paid Shopify orders for sales, and Square booking/payment records for reading revenue. Deduplicate by the provider's order/transaction/appointment identifiers and account for cancellations and refunds. Cross-domain GA4 configuration and Square source retention still require verification; referral tags on Namaste links alone do not prove either.

## Validation

The suite covers static referral tagging; unchanged cart attributes and direct Square links; preserved catalog variant IDs/prices; canonical and sitemap consistency; metadata duplication; preview analytics/indexing suppression; roster notices; click-event semantics and payload sanitization; add-to-cart quantity caps; checkout handoff events; unavailable products; offline variant selection; analytics failure; and mobile menu/cart keyboard behavior.

JavaScript syntax checks, Python compilation and `git diff --check` pass. A separate comparison confirms all pre-existing fields in all 25 stored reader records are unchanged, including rates, schedules, names and direct booking URLs. Only availability notices were added to three records.

Tests use seven synthetic products exclusively in temporary copies. External requests, including Google Analytics, Shopify checkout and Square, are intercepted. They do not replace or prove a build from the full current product catalog. The real product-fetch, price, inventory and deployment workflow was not modified. No customer data was read or changed, and no bookings, purchases, credentials or paid commitments were created.
